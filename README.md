# Seat Watch for Windows

Seat Watch watches BookMyShow and District shows you choose and alerts you on your PC (and phone, if you like)
the moment the seats you want come back, for example when a sold-out show gets cancellations.
It only sends alerts: it never books, holds or pays for anything, and never asks for your BookMyShow or District login.

**Download:** the newest `SeatWatchSetup.exe` is under [Releases](https://github.com/sishirD/seatwatch-releases/releases/latest).

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed yet: choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.

Seat Watch is an independent tool. It isn't made by, endorsed by or connected with BookMyShow, District or Zomato.

`status.json` is read by the app to tell you when a new version is ready.
